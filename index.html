ABOUT PROJECT S:

Software shaped by survey methodology.

Project S: is an umbrella for independent software tools supporting survey research. The project emphasizes transparent workflows, methodological quality and practical usability in research environments.

The Project S: family covers complementary stages of survey data workflows: DE supports straightforward manual data entry, DEVS adds validation and advanced quality-control workflows, QC focuses on multidimensional assessment of survey-data quality and coherence, and Interviewer provides dedicated tools for analysing interviewer behaviour, interviewer effects and fieldwork quality. Additional tools may be added as the project evolves.
